## 1. Project Overview

**Custom Industry Tools** is a growing collection of interactive digital tools, calculators, estimators, diagnostic assistants and practical applications developed to address real-world challenges across different industries.

The repository brings together digital solutions developed through practical project work, industry research, problem-solving and the identification of opportunities to simplify everyday business and operational processes.

The objective is to create a central library of useful, accessible and industry-specific digital resources rather than limiting individual tools to separate websites or projects.

The collection will expand as additional tools and applications are developed.

---

## 2. Purpose & Objectives

The project focuses on developing practical digital solutions that can:

- Simplify industry-specific calculations and processes.
- Assist with equipment selection and technical assessments.
- Support fault identification and troubleshooting.
- Improve operational planning and decision-making.
- Reduce repetitive manual calculations.
- Provide accessible information and guidance.
- Demonstrate how digital tools can address practical business requirements.

The tools are designed with usability, accessibility and practical application in mind.

## 7. Important Information & Limitations

The tools are intended to provide practical assistance, preliminary calculations and decision-support information.

Results should be interpreted in the context of the individual application.

Where professional inspections, technical verification, manufacturer specifications or regulatory compliance are required, users should consult appropriately qualified professionals.

The tools do not replace professional assessments, certified inspections or applicable legal and safety requirements.
